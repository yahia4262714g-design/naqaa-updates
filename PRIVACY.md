# Naqaa — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 24 September 2026_

Naqaa is a personal Windows desktop application that enhances the user's own
short videos on their own computer and, only when the user asks, uploads a
finished video to the user's own TikTok or Instagram account.

## What Naqaa processes
- **Videos you choose.** They are read and processed locally on your computer.
  Naqaa never uploads your videos anywhere except to the TikTok or Instagram
  account you connected, and only when you press «Publish».
- **Account tokens.** When you connect TikTok (through TikTok's official Login
  Kit) or Instagram (through Meta's official Instagram API), Naqaa stores the
  access tokens the platform issues. They are encrypted with Windows' per-user
  protection and kept only on your computer. Naqaa never asks for, sees or
  stores your password.
- **Basic profile information.** Your TikTok display name or Instagram username
  is read only to show which account is connected.

## What Naqaa does not do
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
- No servers of its own: nothing is sent to the developer.
- No data is sold or shared with anyone.

## Removing your data
Choose «Disconnect» in Naqaa's settings to delete the stored tokens, or
uninstall Naqaa. You can also revoke access at any time from your TikTok or
Instagram account settings.
